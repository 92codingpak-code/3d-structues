# Licence for the 3D models in this directory

The `.glb` files in this directory are **derivative works** of
[BodyParts3D](https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/) by the
Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS). They were produced from the
BodyParts3D STL release by `scripts/process_assets.py`, which decimates the
mesh, converts millimetres to metres, rotates Z-up to Y-up, and applies Draco
compression.
